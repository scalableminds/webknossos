### Changed
- The brush is now centered on the hovered voxel for odd brush sizes and on the nearest voxel corner for even brush sizes. This avoids single-voxel bumps at the edges of the brush, and a brush of size 1 paints exactly the hovered voxel. The brush shape no longer depends on the exact mouse position within a voxel when brushing in a coarser mag.

### Fixed
- Fixed that the brush preview was always rendered in mag 1, even if the brush is applied in a coarser mag of the volume layer. The preview now matches the painted voxels.
