### Added
- Added a tool to align the layers of a dataset to one fixed layer by placing matching landmarks side by side, similar to BigWarp. The landmarks of all layers are stored in one alignment annotation, and the resulting transforms can be applied to the dataset. Open it via "Align Layers…" in the context menu of a dataset in the dashboard.
