### Changed
- Layer transforms are now scaled and rotated around the center of the layer instead of the center of the dataset, so that a layer rotates in place. Existing transforms are automatically rebased onto the new pivot, which does not change how the layer is rendered.
- The translation sliders of the layer transform settings now adapt their step size and range to the current zoom level, so that one step corresponds to roughly one screen pixel at any zoom.
- The translation sliders adapt their range when the min/max value is selected so that the maximum translation range is no longer limited.
- The scaling sliders of the layer transform settings now use a finer step size and extend their range when a value at the end of the slider is committed.
