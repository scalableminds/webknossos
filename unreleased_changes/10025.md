### Changed
- Reworked the skeleton rows in the Skeletons tab to match the Segments tab: the number of nodes is shown at the right end of a row, the skeleton actions appear there on hover, the active skeleton is marked with a colored bar, and group rows show how many skeletons they contain.
- Skeletons and skeleton groups are now renamed by double-clicking their name or via the context menu, which also shows the tree ID.
- Grouped the buttons of the Skeletons tab's toolbar with dividers and moved the "more actions" menu to the end of the toolbar.
