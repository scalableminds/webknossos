### Added
- Added bookmarks to annotations. A bookmark stores the current view (position, rotation, zoom, 3D camera, layer settings, mappings and meshes) and can be restored from the bookmarks tab. Merging annotations keeps their bookmarks.

### Migration
- FossilDB now needs to be opened with additional column families `annotationBookmarks` and `annotationBookmarkThumbnails`.
