### Added
- Organization owners and admins are now warned by email when the used storage reaches 90% and 100% of the included storage.
- Users are now notified by email when someone shares an annotation or dataset with their teams, transfers an annotation to them, changes their team memberships or roles, or manually assigns a task to them.
