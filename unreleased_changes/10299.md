### Fixed
- Fixed that after merging volume annotations with segment id remapping, the segment list and segment visibilities of the first annotation could refer to old segment ids that no longer matched the volume data.
- Fixed that the largest segment id could be reset to 0 after importing volume data or merging volume annotations, so that newly brushed segments could reuse existing segment ids.
- Fixed that Zarr3 downloads of volume annotations with a fallback layer were missing buckets in which fallback segments had been erased, so these segments reappeared after re-uploading.
