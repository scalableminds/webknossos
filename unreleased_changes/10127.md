### Added
- Added the "Open Source" and "Enterprise" pricing plans, see https://home.webknossos.org/pricing. Open Source includes the collaboration, project and dataset management features of Team with unlimited users and storage, but no connectomics proofreading, no AI-based quick-select tool, no AI credits and no jobs on WEBKNOSSOS workers (e.g. AI analysis, animations, precomputed meshes, conversions and exports). Enterprise unlocks all features of Power with unlimited users and storage. Open Source organizations on webknossos.org can request an upgrade to the Team or Power plan to get access to jobs and AI features. On self-hosted instances, they are pointed to sales instead.

### Removed
- Removed the "Custom" pricing plan. Existing organizations on the Custom plan are migrated to the Enterprise plan. Organizations created on self-hosted instances now get the Open Source plan.
