### Fixed
- Fixed that the brush's autofill no longer filled the area enclosed by a stroke that is released near its start.
- Fixed that erasing with the brush in the "overwrite empty" mode erased nothing instead of only the active segment, and that the hint to change the overwrite mode no longer appeared when a brush stroke changed no voxels.
- Fixed that erasing with a single click of the brush caused an error.

### Changed
- Erasing with the brush no longer fills the area enclosed by the stroke, so that erasing along a segment's outline only removes the stroke instead of the whole segment. [#4624](https://github.com/scalableminds/webknossos/issues/4624)
