### Changed
- Layer transforms are now scaled and rotated around the center of the layer instead of the center of the dataset, so that a layer rotates in place. Existing transforms are automatically rebased onto the new pivot, which does not change how the layer is rendered.
- The translation sliders of the layer transform settings are now relative: their handle rests in the center and dragging it increases or decreases the translation by up to one viewport extent, labeled at both ends of the slider. On release the handle returns to the center, so that the translation is not limited by the slider's range and the reachable precision follows the zoom level.
- The scaling sliders of the layer transform settings are relative as well: they work in log scale and reach from a tenth of the current size up to ten times it, so shrinking and enlarging a layer are symmetric gestures.
