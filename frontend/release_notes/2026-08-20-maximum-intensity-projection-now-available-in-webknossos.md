---
title: "Maximum Intensity Projection now available in WEBKNOSSOS 🚀"
---

MIP projects a data layer through the volume and displays the highest intensity value along each ray: a fast and intuitive way to see 3D structures without segmentation or precomputation. It works across light microscopy, volume EM , and correlative datasets, so you can explore your data in 3D directly in the browser.

Read the article: <https://home.webknossos.org/blog/mip>
