### Added
- Added the "Open-Source" and "Enterprise" pricing plans, see https://home.webknossos.org/pricing. Open-Source includes the collaboration, project and dataset management features of Team with unlimited users and storage, but no connectomics proofreading, no AI-based quick-select tool, no AI credits and no jobs on WEBKNOSSOS workers (e.g. AI analysis, animations, precomputed meshes, conversions and exports). Enterprise unlocks all features of Power with unlimited users and storage.

### Removed
- Removed the "Custom" pricing plan. Existing organizations on the Custom plan are migrated to the Enterprise plan, which is now also the default for organizations created on self-hosted instances.
