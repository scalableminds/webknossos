### Added
- Added OME-Zarr (`.ozx`) as an export format in the TIFF and OME-Zarr export tab of the download dialog.

### Changed
- `POST /jobs/run/exportTiff` now takes an `exportFormat` parameter (`ome_tiff` | `tiff_stack` | `ozx`) instead of the `asOmeTiff` boolean.

### Migration
- The `export_tiff` job now passes an `export_format` argument to the worker. Deploy a webknossos-worker that accepts it (voxelytics#4795) before or together with this release; older workers fail on the unknown argument.
