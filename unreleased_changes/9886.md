### Added
- Organizations whose pricing plan is about to expire now get a reminder email. It is sent to the organization owner and all admins 30, 14 and 7 days before the expiry date and contains the plan name, the expiry date and a link to renew. The lead times can be configured via `webKnossos.pricingPlanExpiryReminder.leadTimesDays`, and the reminders can be turned off via `webKnossos.pricingPlanExpiryReminder.enabled`.
