### Changed
- In the live transform settings of a layer (opened from the layer list in the left sidebar), layer transforms are now scaled and rotated around the center of the layer instead of the center of the dataset, so that a layer rotates in place. Existing transforms are automatically rebased onto the new pivot, which does not change how the layer is rendered. The rotation settings of the dataset settings are not affected.
- The translation sliders of the live transform settings are now relative: their handle rests in the center and dragging it increases or decreases the translation by up to one viewport extent, labeled at both ends of the slider. On release the handle returns to the center, so that the translation is not limited by the slider's range and the reachable precision follows the zoom level.
- The scaling sliders of the live transform settings are relative as well: they reach from a tenth of the current size up to ten times it. A lock toggle next to each scaling slider makes the locked axes scale together and keep their proportions. All axes are locked by default, so that scaling is uniform unless an axis is unlocked.
