### Changed
- Combined the separate upgrade request dialogs (plan, users, storage, AI Add-on, credits, plan extension) into one dialog. Several upgrades and a note can be requested at once, and sales receives them in a single email.

### Removed
- Removed the routes `/api/pricing/requestExtension`, `/api/pricing/requestUpgrade`, `/api/pricing/requestUsers`, `/api/pricing/requestStorage`, `/api/pricing/requestCredits` and `/api/pricing/requestAiAddon` in favor of `POST /api/pricing/upgradeRequest`.
